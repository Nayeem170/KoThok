Settings

Tap the gear icon at the top of the screen to open Settings.

Display: font size, frontlight brightness, and an auto-sleep timer that turns the screen off after a few minutes of inactivity.

Audio: voice, reading speed, and volume for read-aloud.

Connectivity: turn WiFi and Bluetooth on or off. The dots show the status: black is off, red is connecting, green is connected.

Updates: KoThok checks GitHub for a newer release when WiFi is up. Turn this off if you prefer.
