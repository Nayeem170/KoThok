Listening (Read-Aloud)

KoThok can read the book aloud. Tap the play button at the bottom of the screen to start, and tap it again to pause.

Read-aloud needs two things: WiFi (for the voice service) and Bluetooth headphones or a speaker. If the play button does nothing, check that both are on in Settings.

While listening, the disk on screen shows the playback position. KoThok turns pages automatically as the narration moves on.

You can still swipe left and right while listening. Swiping does not stop the audio.
