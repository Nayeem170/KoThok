Reading vs Audio Mode

KoThok has two modes. Tap the mode toggle in the header to switch between them.

Reading mode is silent. You read the page yourself and turn pages by swiping. The footer shows the progress bar and page number.

Audio mode reads aloud. The footer shows the play button and the vinyl disk tracks playback. Pages turn on their own as the narration advances.

Both modes share the same sleep timer. In reading mode with the timer off, the screen stays on until you press the power button.
