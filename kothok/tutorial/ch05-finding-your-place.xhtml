Finding Your Place

Tap the chapters icon (top right) to open the table of contents. The list shows the book's chapters and sections, indented by depth. Tap any entry to jump there.

To save a spot, tap the bookmark icon in the header. A marker is set at the first line of the current page.

To return to a saved bookmark, tap the jump icon next to the bookmark. You can have one bookmark per book.

KoThok also remembers where you were when you close a book. Opening it again puts you back on the same page.
