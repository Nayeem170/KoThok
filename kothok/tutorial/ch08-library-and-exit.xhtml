Your Library and Leaving

Tap the library icon (top left) to see all your books. The library scans the entire Kobo storage for EPUB files.

Double-tap a book cover to open it. Single tap just highlights it.

The pills above the grid filter books by script: Latin, Bangla, Arabic, and so on. Tap a pill to narrow the list.

To leave KoThok, tap the exit button (top right, in the library view). KoThok closes and the Kobo reboots back to its home screen. This is normal: returning to the Kobo software cleanly requires a restart.
